### 🚀 About Me

I'm a developer passionate about **low‑level architecture** and **modern full‑stack engineering**.  
I love exploring how things work from the silicon up, while building clean, scalable applications with **React**, **TypeScript**, and **Node.js**.

- 🔭 I’m currently working on **Conception et Simulation d'un Processeur** – a deep dive into CPU design and simulation.
- 🌱 I’m constantly learning about **compilers**, **VLSI**, and **cloud‑native patterns**.
- 👯 I’m open to collaborating on **open‑source tooling** for developers.
- 💬 Ask me about **React**, **TypeScript**, **system design**, or **processor microarchitecture**.
- ⚡ Fun fact: I believe every great developer should understand what happens between `code` and `clock cycles`.
